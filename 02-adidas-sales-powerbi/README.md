# Adidas Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## Problem
Track Adidas sales performance across retailers, regions, products
and time, and find what drives sales and profit.

## Tools Used
Power BI Desktop, DAX

## What I Did
- Created DAX measures: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin,
  Total Units Sold, Average Price per Unit
- Built KPI cards, a monthly sales trend, a state-wise map,
  a region-wise donut chart, and product-wise and retailer-wise bar charts
- Added month, sales method and year buttons for interactive filtering
- Designed the layout with Adidas branding and a Reset button

## Key Insights
- Total sales of $899.90M with a profit of $332.13M (42.30% margin)
- West Gear is the top retailer with $242.96M in sales
- Men's Street Footwear is the best-selling product ($208.83M)
- West is the largest region by share of sales
- Sales peak in July and are lowest in March
